## Scale and pass rule
- Each check is binary: `1` = PASS as stated, `0` = anything else, including NOT-RUN. T_accept 1, T_dim 1, T_reject 0.
- **Cap: 3 E-DI iterations.** The packet says `cap_counts: generations`, where one generation = one iteration (brief `proposed.cap_definition`). Maker calls and retries inside an iteration are limited only by the money bound, the clock-time bound, and the **tripwire** (Owner standing rule, 2026-10-28 15:52 EDT): after the same Maker failure class 3 times in a row within an iteration, the Maker stops and reports to the Orchestrator so the request can be changed. A reply with `finish_reason` `length` and empty content is a failure class of its own: stop, don't retry. Every attempt is logged with its `finish_reason`. A void batch doesn't use up an iteration.
- The job passes when every check scores 1. A 0 means a new iteration if below the cap, or `needs_owner` at the cap.
- Money: < US$5 in total, enforced in the harness (approval item 6).
- Time: ends **2026-10-29 00:34 EDT** (UTC-4; 2026-10-29T04:34:00Z), which is open 2026-10-28 16:34:22 EDT rounded down to the minute + 8 h. No model call may be sent at or after it.
- Breaching either bound sends the job to `needs_owner`.
